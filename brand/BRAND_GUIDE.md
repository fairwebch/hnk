# HNK Kroatien Schwyz — Brand Guide v1.0

## Brand direction

**Premium Editorial Sport**

HNK Kroatien Schwyz should feel athletic, energetic, modern, authentic and professionally controlled. The visual identity combines Croatian heritage, Swiss context, football culture and community without relying on stereotypical football-poster aesthetics.

## Brand characteristics

- athletic
- premium
- authentic
- modern
- Croatian, without visual clichés
- Swiss in clarity and structure
- community-driven
- recognisable beyond the logo

## Core principle

If a design starts to look like a generic AI, Canva or amateur football flyer, simplify it. Do not add more effects.

## Color approach

### Primary accent
- Croatia Red `#D8232F`
- Croatia Dark `#B4121E`

Red is an accent, normally around 5–15% of the composition. It is used for CTA, date emphasis, kickers, lines and small graphic blocks.

### Navy system
- `#070E1D`
- `#0A1428`
- `#0D1B33`
- `#0F213F`
- `#122444`
- `#16274A`

Primary navy is `#0D1B33`.

### Light system
- Paper `#F5F6FA`
- White `#FFFFFF`
- Line `#E4E8F0`

Dark-first and light-first compositions are both valid. The choice depends on photography, event mood and hierarchy.

## Typography

Only the official Barlow system is used.

### Barlow Condensed
For:
- display headlines
- event titles
- kickers
- CTA labels
- categories
- large date treatments

Weights: 500, 600, 700, 800.

Large event titles are usually uppercase and may use italic when appropriate.

### Barlow
For:
- body copy
- time and location
- contact information
- secondary information
- URLs

Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700.

No additional display font should be introduced without approval.

## Logo system

### Primary logo
`public/assets/logo.svg`

Used when the club identity must be explicit: official events, tournaments, invitations, print and sponsor material.

### Secondary mark
`public/assets/logo-mark.svg`

Approved as a secondary mark for controlled uses such as watermark, social marker, background detail, avatar, story and small-format brand element.

The secondary mark does not replace the primary logo in official contexts.

### Planned mono variants
- White mono
- Navy mono

These are technical adaptations only. Geometry and proportions must not be redesigned.

## Photography

Real HNK photography has priority.

Use real:
- players
- teams
- events
- supporters
- club members

AI-generated people are not allowed.

The photographic direction is **Premium Editorial**:
- strong crop
- controlled contrast
- selective darkening
- subtle grain
- masking and layering
- navy toning when useful
- restrained red toning

Photography must remain authentic and recognisable as a real HNK moment.

## Photo vs typography

Strong photo: photography may lead the composition.

Weak or average photo: typography and layout lead.

Do not rescue weak photography with excessive effects.

## Grid

Use a **Dynamic Editorial Grid**.

Allowed:
- controlled asymmetry
- large type blocks
- off-grid image crops
- layering
- carefully broken grid moments

Not allowed: visual chaos without a clear structural hierarchy.

## Negative space

Use a medium amount of negative space: enough to feel premium, but not so much that the design loses sports energy.

## Geometry

Sharp corners are the standard.

Avoid rounded app-style cards as a brand default.

The brand geometry is based on:
- sharp edges
- thin lines
- diagonal cuts
- restrained skew elements

## Signature element: skew / diagonal

The primary graphic signature is the diagonal/skew language, inspired by the website's `skewX(-8deg)` treatment.

Use it sparingly: normally 1–2 significant diagonal or skew elements per composition.

## Checkerboard

The Croatian checkerboard is a secondary micro-signature only.

Allowed:
- thin strip
- small corner
- separator
- subtle pattern detail

Not allowed:
- large checkerboard background
- dominant frame
- full-poster decoration
- automatic use on every design

If the checkerboard is one of the first things noticed, it is probably too strong.

## Croatian / Swiss symbols

Flags and national symbols are not standard decoration. Use only when the content specifically justifies them.

Identity should primarily come from the HNK logo, palette, typography, photography and layout system.

## Texture and effects

Allowed in moderation:
- grain
- noise
- paper texture
- subtle sports-surface texture
- controlled blur
- masking
- overlays
- layering

Effects must support composition, never become the composition.

## Shadows

Use discreetly for separation and depth only.

CTA shadow reference:
`0 8px 20px rgba(216,35,47,.3)`

No neon or gaming-style glow.

## Lines and borders

Use 1–2px editorial lines by default. Thicker borders are reserved for emphasis.

## Event hierarchy

The two main elements of an event flyer are:

1. Event name
2. Date

Secondary information:
- location
- time
- CTA
- URL/contact when needed

## Copy amount

Main flyers are minimal.

Standard content:
- event name
- date
- time
- location
- CTA
- web/contact if needed

Longer information belongs on the website, caption or a separate info post.

## CTA

The standard CTA points to the website.

Use either:
- `kroatien-schwyz.ch`
- a short event URL when available

QR code is not standard and is used only when specifically requested.

## Sponsors

Sponsors are clean and discreet.

Standard:
- dedicated sponsor zone
- usually near the bottom
- equal optical logo height
- enough whitespace
- separated from the main event hierarchy

Contractual sponsor requirements override visual preference when necessary.

## Languages

Use HR or DE depending on the audience.

For major public events, separate HR and DE versions are preferred over forcing both languages onto one flyer.

German uses Swiss spelling, e.g. `Fussball` rather than `Fußball`.

## Modular system

HNK does not use one identical template for every event.

Each event may have its own art direction while retaining:
- typography
- palette
- logo rules
- grid logic
- photography treatment
- geometry
- signature details
- information hierarchy

## Formats

The system covers:
- Instagram feed
- Instagram square
- Story / Reels cover
- Facebook
- WhatsApp
- web banners and hero images
- A4/A3 print
- posters and flyers
- roll-ups
- event banners
- LED/digital signage
- sponsor walls

Each format must be recomposed, not merely cropped.

## Canva

Canva is the operational editable-template environment.

Templates are strictly controlled. Users may normally change only:
- text
- date/time
- photography
- sponsor logo

Grid, fonts, colors, logo position, spacing, signature elements and sponsor zone remain locked.

## Approval workflow

Every new visual follows:

1. Brief
2. Missing-information check
3. One best visual direction
4. Danijel approval
5. Design
6. Brand review
7. Export/adaptation

## Brand exceptions

Any deliberate exception requires Danijel's approval first.

This includes new fonts, new colors, strong checkerboard use, dominant red, new logo variants, new visual language or any other departure from this system.
