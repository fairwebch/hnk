## Tracking

- `wider2`: `0.12em`
- `widest2`: `0.22em`
- `widest3`: `0.32em`

Use expanded tracking mainly for kickers, labels and short uppercase elements. Avoid excessive tracking on long headlines.

## Geometry

- default radius: `0`
- sharp corners are the visual standard
- primary diagonal reference: `-8deg` skew
- typical composition limit: 1–2 significant skew/diagonal elements

## Shadow

### CTA reference
`0 8px 20px rgba(216,35,47,.3)`

Keep campaign shadows restrained.

## Checkerboard

Production token:

`repeating-conic-gradient(#D8232F 0% 25%, #FFFFFF 0% 50%) 0 0 / 16px 16px`

Brand application rule: use as a micro-detail only. The existence of the token does not authorize large checkerboard surfaces.

## Visual usage guidance

- Red usually ~5–15% of a composition.
- Dark and light compositions are equally valid.
- Avoid introducing additional palette colors unless approved.
- Texture, blur and shadows are supportive tokens, not dominant brand elements.
